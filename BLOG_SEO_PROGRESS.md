## 3. Blog Optimization Checklist (Per-Page Execution)

For each blog post, the following criteria must be met and verified:
- [ ] **A. Keyword & Intent:** Primary keyword naturally in Title, H1, first 100 words, meta description, and 1-2 H2s.
- [ ] **B. Competitor Gaps:** Subtopics, actionable frameworks, and verified regulatory standards exceeding top ranking competitors.
- [ ] **C. Content Structure:** Answer-first intro (40–60 words), Key Takeaways summary box, Table of Contents with jump anchors, logical H2/H3 hierarchy, grade 6-8 readability, FAQ section from People Also Ask.
- [ ] **D. E-E-A-T Signals:** Real agency lessons/mistakes, visible author byline linking to Saim's real bio, official regulatory citations (FSA, Gov.uk, W3C), published & last updated dates, BlogPosting + Person + BreadcrumbList + FAQPage JSON-LD.
- [ ] **E. Unique Elements:** Original interactive tool integration, worked calculation examples, checklists, decision matrices.
- [ ] **F. Helpful Media:** Explanatory diagrams, tables, infographics, responsive images with WebP/lazy-loading and descriptive British English alt text.
- [ ] **G. Silo Navigation:** Upward link to `/blogs`, sibling links via `BlogSiloNav`, deep links to relevant tools/services.
- [ ] **H. Technical Quality & CWV:** British English spelling, evergreen URL, valid canonical, zero 404 links, LCP ≤ 2.5s, CLS ≤ 0.1.
- [ ] **I. Verification & Commit:** Clean build, CI quality guards pass, committed and tracked.
