## 3. Pain Points & Problems Solved
- **The "Agency Markup" Trap:** Traditional web agencies charge £5,000 to £15,000+, take 3 to 6 months, route communication through non-technical account managers, and lock clients into expensive ongoing maintenance retainers.
- **The "Bloated Template" Problem:** DIY builders (Wix, Squarespace) and bloated WordPress themes load dozens of third-party plugins, score below 40 on mobile PageSpeed, frustrate visitors, and kill Google search rankings.
- **The "Disappearing Freelancer" Risk:** Cheap marketplace freelancers often vanish mid-project, deliver broken responsive layouts, introduce security vulnerabilities, and leave undocumented code.
- **The Solution at Saim Dev:** Direct access to senior full-stack engineers, modern sub-second Next.js and headless stacks, transparent fixed pricing, delivery in 5 to 14 business days, and complete code ownership.

## 4. Competitive Landscape
- **Direct Competitors:**
  1. *Mid-tier UK Web Agencies* (e.g. Lounge, Web Choice, Kyan, Blue Frontier) — High authority, established portfolios, but expensive (£5k–£25k), slow turnaround (8–16 weeks), heavy retainer models.
  2. *Boutique Next.js & Jamstack Studios* (e.g. Unfold, Heavyweight, Flightpath) — High technical quality, modern aesthetic, but premium pricing and often ignore small business CMS/Shopify needs.
  3. *Freelance Web Developers & Upwork/Fiverr Top-Rated Pros* — Lower price point, but variable reliability, fragmented skill sets (great design OR great code, rarely both), lack of comprehensive SEO/copywriting capabilities.
- **Secondary & Indirect Competitors:**
  - Self-serve Website Builders (Webflow, Squarespace, Wix, Shopify native themes).
  - Offshore development agencies.
