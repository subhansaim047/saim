How long does a website or web app project take?

High-converting landing pages and takeaway platforms (£250) or starter websites (£390) are delivered in 5 to 7 business days. Full Business Flagship websites (£790) take 7 to 14 days, and custom e-commerce stores or web applications typically ship in 2 to 4 weeks.

How does your $0 Upfront Prototype Guarantee work?

You never pay blind. Share your project brief or a reference website you love on WhatsApp or via our Contact page, and we engineer a live interactive prototype first.

Once you review the live preview and love the direction, we lock in the fixed milestone price and complete the full build.

How do you communicate and manage development?

We keep communication direct, fast, and transparent via WhatsApp, Slack, or email—with live staging links so you can test your website in real time as it comes together. You work directly with the senior engineer building your project, with zero account-manager handoffs.

How is Saim Dev different from a traditional UK agency or WordPress builder?

Traditional agencies charge £5,000–£15,000+ to cover account managers and overhead while outsourcing work to juniors or slow WordPress templates. At Saim Dev, you work directly with senior Next.js, React, and Shopify engineers. We deliver 99+ Google PageSpeed scores, bespoke UI/UX, and 100% source code ownership at transparent fixed prices starting from £250.

What tech stack do you use to build websites and web apps?

We engineer bespoke marketing sites, business platforms, and web applications using Next.js 15, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, GSAP/Motion, and Node.js. For e-commerce and content management, we build on Shopify, headless CMS platforms (Sanity, Strapi), or custom full-stack ordering engines.

How does payment work?

We never bill unpredictable hourly rates. You approve the fixed price and scope upfront, and after seeing your initial prototype, payments are split into simple milestones—with zero mandatory monthly retainers.

Do I own 100% of the source code and website?

Yes—100%. Unlike agencies that lock you into proprietary platforms or mandatory monthly fees, upon completion we hand over the full GitHub repository, source code, custom domain setup, and cloud deployment so your business owns every asset outright.

Can you clone or rebuild a reference website 1:1 in custom code?

Yes! If you have found a website whose layout, animations, and aesthetic you love, we can engineer a 1:1 custom-coded Next.js rebuild tailored to your brand, colours, logo, and SEO—without slow third-party page builders.

Do you include technical SEO and mobile speed optimization?

Every website we ship includes complete technical SEO architecture out of the box: semantic H1–H3 hierarchy, JSON-LD structured data schemas, XML sitemaps, OpenGraph tags, canonical URLs, and Core Web Vitals optimization targeting 95–100 PageSpeed scores.
