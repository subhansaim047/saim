Specifications

Share your design idea and place the order, and our team will guide you through the entire customization process.

Once your order is received, our design team will review your requirements and contact you to confirm the details. We will create a design mockup for approval, discuss materials, colors, sizing, and any personalization such as logos, embroidery, or patches. After your approval, the jacket will move into production.


How It Works

  1. Place Your Order – Submit your request and upload your design or describe your idea.

  2. Design Consultation – Our team contacts you to finalize details.

  3. Mockup Approval – You review and approve the design before production.

  4. Production & Delivery – Your jacket is handcrafted and shipped after quality checks.

 

Size & Fit
  • Following your purchase, your design consultant will reach out to guide you through a bespoke measurement process, ensuring an impeccable fit tailored to you.
  • If you wish to replicate the fit and detailing of a jacket you already own, simply share the request with us via email — we’ll take care of the rest.

Shipping & Delivery
  • This product is made-to-order and requires approximately 10–12 weeks for completion. If you have a specific delivery timeline in mind, please email us with your preferred date, and we’ll do our best to accommodate.
  • We offer free express shipping worldwide.

The Process

How It Works

Every jacket begins with your idea and is shaped through a guided process, ensuring comfort, confidence, and complete clarity from start to finish.

1. Order Placement

Place your order with essential customizations and share your design vision with us, so we can clearly understand what you’d like us to create for you.

2. Design Consultation & Review

 A dedicated design consultant is assigned to you, carefully reviewing your order, suggesting thoughtful refinements or add-ons, and confirming every detail with you before proceeding.

3. Illustration Creation

We create a hyper-realistic visualization of your design and share it with you, so you can review, suggest changes, and feel confident before production begins.

4. Measurements & Verification

Your consultant guides you through the measurement process with simple instructions and video guides, ensuring accuracy and carefully verifying everything before moving into production.

5. Production

Once everything is approved, your custom leather jacket moves into production, where a skilled craftsman brings your design to life with precision, care, and close attention to detail.

6. Photoshoot Sharing

 After completion, we conduct a photoshoot of your finished custom leather jacket and share the images with you, so you can review every detail before final approval.

7. Approval & Delivery

Once you’re fully satisfied with the final piece, your jacket is carefully packed and dispatched, ensuring it reaches you safely and exactly as expected.
