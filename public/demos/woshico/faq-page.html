Questions

Frequently asked
‍questions

01. What does 'We Come To You' mean?

Luxury Wash USA is a fully mobile auto spa. You don't need to drive anywhere—we bring our premium car washing and detailing services directly to your home, driveway, or office.
02. Do I need to provide water or electricity?

Not at all! Our mobile detailing units are fully equipped with our own water supply and power generators. We just need the keys and your car.
03. How do I book an appointment?

You can book easily by clicking the 'Book Now' button anywhere on our website, which will take you directly to our Setmore scheduling page.
04. What is included in your luxury detailing?

We offer comprehensive services ranging from exterior washes and tire shining to deep interior vacuuming, leather conditioning, and waxing. Check our Services page for full details.
05. Do you offer subscription or membership plans?

Yes! We are a subscription-based mobile car wash company. We offer regular maintenance plans so your vehicle stays spotless all year round without you having to think about it.
