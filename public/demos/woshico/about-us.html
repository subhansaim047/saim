The Bentley Brandon Story

Bentley Brandon has been the leading expert in the car wash industry. By being recognized by (2) former President Obama, and President Trump. Through Bentley Brandon efforts to impact the community, Elite Detailing USA has served the community all year round from Teachers to leaders in the community.

With recent events such as the global pandemic, Bentley Brandon has decided to make Elite Detailing a subscription-based Car Wash company. This initiative helps keep families and drivers safe and their vehicles clean every month or bimonthly.

Through Elite Detailing, Bentley offers fast, friendly, and efficient detailing services, with an unmatched level of luxury!
