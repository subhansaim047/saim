Luxury Wash USA & Bentley Brandon

Bentley Brandon first began working for his last employer at the age of thirteen. Over the next several years, Bentley learned as much about the business as he could, and eventually moved into management when he was only eighteen years old. When the previous owner decided to sell the business in the fall of 2010, Bentley took advantage of the opportunity to operate his own company, at the age of twenty-three, and Luxury Wash was born!

Bentley Brandon has been the leading expert in the car wash industry. By being recognized by (2) former President Obama, and President Trump. Through Bentley Brandon efforts to impact the community, Luxury Wash USA has served the community all year round from Teachers to leaders in the community.

With recent events such as the global pandemic, Bentley Brandon has decided to make Luxury Wash a subscription-based Car Wash company. This initiative helps keep families and drivers safe and their vehicles clean every month or bimonthly.

Through Luxury Wash, Bentley offers fast, friendly, and efficient detailing services, with an unmatched level of luxury! Our company is quickly becoming recognized in the world as the “luxury car wash” car wash in the world. In fact, Bentley and Luxury Wash have become so popular that all types of celebrities have acknowledged him after learning about Bentley’s amazing story!

No matter where you are! Luxury Wash “Brings Luxury to every vehicle in the world.”
