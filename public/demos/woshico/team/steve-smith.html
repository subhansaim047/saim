Personal information

A car wash team member performs exterior and interior vehicle cleaning, applies waxes and protective coatings, maintains the cleanliness of the facility and equipment, and provides excellent customer service by greeting clients, explaining services, and processing payments. Team members must follow safety procedures, work efficiently to meet targets, and collaborate with colleagues to ensure high-quality results and safe environmentWash and dry vehicle exteriors, vacuum and clean interiors, and apply waxes or protective coatings.

Service advisors manage customer repairs and communication. In production, team leaders and members ensure quality standards are met and problems are solved. Detailers ensure vehicles look their best by cleaning them thoroughly before sale or customer pickup.
