How do I book?

Simply visit our booking page online, choose your service, pick a time that suits you, and confirm.

Is consultation really free always?

Yes — every appointment at LIANA includes a complimentary one-on-one consultation with your assigned master stylist.

What happens if I need changes?

If you are unhappy with your result we will fix it at no additional charge whatsoever.

How long do appointments take?

It depends on the service — cuts take 45 minutes while colour services can take up to three hours.

Can I bring a photo for reference?

Absolutely — we encourage reference photos and use them as a starting point for your personalised consultation.

Do you take walk-ins?

We welcome walk-ins based on availability but recommend booking in advance to secure your preferred stylist and time.

What products do you use exactly?

We use only professional grade brands including Kérastase, Olaplex, and Redken across every single service we offer.
