Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our luxury on-demand mobile day spa & beauty salon services.

Does the amount I pay for my Spa or Beauty Service include gratuity?

Gratuity is not included in the standard service prices. Tips are welcomed and appreciated directly by our mobile beauticians and massage therapists.

Do we have to put pets in another room during our appointment?

For safety, sanitation, and to allow the specialist to set up treatment tables and equipment without interruption, we recommend keeping pets in a separate room.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept major credit cards, debit cards, and electronic invoice payments. Invoices are issued after confirming booking details and must be settled in advance per policy.

Is it ok that I have my kids around during my appointment?

Yes, having children at home is fine; however, for chemical hair treatments, hot styling tools, or relaxing massages/facials, a quiet, distraction-free space is recommended.

Are the prices the same as going to the salon?

Our prices reflect a luxury on-demand mobile service where licensed professionals bring all equipment, products, and salon-grade setup directly to your private location, saving travel time and ensuring complete privacy.

Do you offer acrylics?

Yes, we offer Acrylic Nails + Gel Polish + Design ($175, 1 hr). Standard regular manicures do not include removal of existing acrylics or SNS unless arranged in advance.

What safety measures ensure protection from fungal/bacterial infections?

Our mobile specialists follow hospital-grade sterilization protocols, using sanitized and disinfected tools, disposable liners and buffers, and strict hygiene standards between clients.

What does your mobile service offer that is different than a traditional salon?

Complete privacy, no waiting rooms, personalized one-on-one attention, customized group or event scheduling, and the comfort of receiving treatments directly in your living room, hotel suite, or office.

How long should my appointment take?

Durations vary based on chosen services, ranging from 15–30 minutes for express treatments to 1–3 hours for full body massages, bridal packages, perms, and multi-step coloring.

What cities in Arizona do you provide mobile services to?

We serve Phoenix, Scottsdale, Paradise Valley, Peoria, Chandler, Ahwatukee, Mesa, Tucson, Flagstaff, Prescott, Sedona, and surrounding Maricopa County communities (along with hubs in CA, NV, and TN).

What safety precautions do you follow?

All technicians are state-licensed, adhere to Covid-19 sanitation guidelines, use clean and sanitized implements, and require clients to provide clean space, lighting, electricity, and water access.

Can I get my Manicure, Pedicure and/or Hair services done outdoors?

Yes, weather permitting and provided there is adequate shade, lighting, a flat surface, an electrical outlet, and nearby water access.
