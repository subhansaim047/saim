Manicures & Pedicures® Body Contouring helps you Slim Your Contours by Freezing Away the Fat

Many men and women struggle to lose that final pocket of fat on their body, whether it is in their stomach, attached to their hip, or glued to their thighs.

While diet and exercise offer effective means for fat reduction and are necessary for a healthy lifestyle, they cannot always target these fat pockets, leaving men and women disappointed with their physical contours.

While plastic surgery procedures like liposuction can remove unwanted fat, not everyone needs invasive surgery to target their remaining fatty deposits. Some patients may benefit more from non-surgical body contouring treatments like Manicures & Pedicures®.

Is Manicures & Pedicures® Right for Me?

Manicures & Pedicures® targets excess fat deposits on healthy individuals who are near to their ideal weight. Manicures & Pedicures® is a body sculpting treatment, not a weight-loss procedure. Patients will notice visibly slimmer contours; however, they will not see a dramatic reduction on the scale.

Patients looking for more correction should consider natural weight loss, weight-loss surgery (such as gastric bypass), or surgical body contouring procedures like liposuction.
