Leadership & Heritage

25+ Years Of Bringing Beauty, Dignity & Comfort To Every Client

Beauticians On The Go was founded by **Carla Olivares** with a deep passion for accessible luxury. Graduating with a double major in Business and Communications from Arizona State University, Carla spent over 17 years refining salon craftsmanship into a mobile enterprise.

Alongside **Jose Olivares** (COO and MBA in International Business from Keller Graduate School), the company has grown into a trusted multi-state provider servicing Arizona, Napa Valley, Las Vegas, and Nashville.

Our Compassionate Care Mission

Beyond weddings and red carpets, we specialize in sensitive in-home salon visits for seniors with Alzheimer's/dementia, oncology patients in pediatric recovery, and individuals in assisted living.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our mobile service policies and setup requirements.

All we require from you is an open floor space for our professional tables/chairs, good room lighting, access to an electrical wall outlet, and access to warm running water. We bring all professional products, linens, sterilized tools, and accessories.
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover) processed securely via Square invoice. We also accept corporate checks for company events. Gratuity may be added to your invoice or paid in cash directly to your stylists.
Your safety is our top priority. We use hospital-grade UV sterilizers (253.7nm wavelength) to disinfect metal implements, which are sealed in sterile pouches until opened in front of you. Nail files, buffers, and pedicure bowl liners are 100% single-use and disposable.
Yes, for individual home appointments we have a 2-service minimum ($150 total service value) to dispatch our mobile team. For group spa parties and bachelorette packages, group sizes typically start at 3 to 6 guests.
Individual appointments require at least 5 hours notice for cancellation to receive a refund (minus processing fee). Group events require a 50% non-refundable deposit at least 7 days in advance to reserve your team of stylists.
