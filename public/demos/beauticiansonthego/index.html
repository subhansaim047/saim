About Beauticians On The Go

Where Beauty Meets Convenience • 25+ Years Experience

Beauticians On The Go, PLLC is an upscale, woman-owned mobile day spa and beauty salon enterprise that brings licensed specialists and professional equipment directly to clients' doors. Founded 17 years ago by Carla Olivares (ASU '16) alongside COO Jose Olivares (MBA '07), we deliver unparalleled convenience and compassionate care from age 4 to 100+.

We cater to private residences, vacation rentals (Airbnb & VRBO), luxury hotels, corporate employee wellness days, wedding venues, hospitals, assisted living, and hospice facilities across Arizona, California, Nevada, and Tennessee.

  • 100% Fully Equipped Mobile Setup (treatment tables, products & hospital-grade sanitation)
  • State-Licensed Specialists with 5 to 15 Years of Salon & Spa Experience
  • Inclusive Care: Bridal Glam, Kids Diva Parties, Corporate Wellness & Senior Care
  • Multi-State Hubs: Phoenix, Scottsdale, Sedona, Los Angeles, Las Vegas & Nashville
