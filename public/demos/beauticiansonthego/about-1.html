Where Beauty Meets Convenience

Beauticians On The Go, PLLC is an upscale, woman-owned mobile day spa and on-site beauty salon enterprise operating for over 17 years with 25+ years of combined industry expertise.

Founded by Carla Olivares (ASU Business & Communications '16) alongside COO Jose Olivares (MBA in International Business '07), our mission is to eliminate the stress of travel, waiting rooms, and rigid salon schedules by delivering licensed beauty specialists, treatment tables, and hospital-grade sterilized implements directly to clients' doors.

We serve private residences, luxury vacation rentals (Airbnb & VRBO), resort hotels, corporate offices, wedding venues, hospitals, assisted living, and hospice facilities across Arizona, California, Nevada, and Tennessee.

Hospital-Grade Safety

We use sterilized tools, single-use liners, disposable buffers, and hospital-grade disinfectants between every single appointment.

Licensed Specialists

Every cosmetologist, massage therapist, and esthetician on our team is state-licensed with 5 to 15+ years of hands-on salon experience.

Compassionate Care

We proudly provide specialized, gentle personal care for elderly, bedridden, dementia, and hospice clients with the utmost dignity and respect.

Who We Proudly Serve

Delivering on-site luxury and professional beauty services across diverse client communities from age 4 to 100+.

Private Homes & Rentals

Transform your living room, patio, or Airbnb / VRBO vacation rental into a private wellness sanctuary with zero commute.

Bridal & Bachelorette

Complete bridal party glam, camera-ready makeup, updos, lashes, and mani/pedi party packages on location.

Corporate Wellness

Boost workplace morale with seated chair massages, express manicures, and employee appreciation relaxation events.

Kids Diva & Teens

Exciting birthday party packages with gentle hairstyles, sparkles, glitter makeup, and mini mani/pedis for ages 4–17.

Seniors & Assisted Living

Gentle, patient haircuts, styling, and foot/hand care for seniors, assisted living residents, and dementia patients.

Hospice & Hospital Care

Empathetic bedside grooming and relaxation care tailored to comfort bedridden individuals and hospice patients.
