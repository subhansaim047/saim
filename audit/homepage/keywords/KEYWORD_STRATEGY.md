## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Positioning
The homepage of **Saim Dev** has undergone a fundamental repositioning: from an overly narrow, low-tier positioning (*"Next.js Websites for UK Small Businesses – From £250"*) to an authoritative, full-spectrum digital engineering studio targeting high-intent commercial buyers across the UK and English-speaking global markets.

### The Problem with Previous Positioning:
- **Artificial Cap on Deal Size:** Restricting headline positioning to "£250 small business websites" actively repelled higher-budget clients seeking custom e-commerce, web applications, SaaS MVPs, and modern corporate re-platforming.
- **Ignored 80% of Agency Capability:** While Saim Dev excels at Next.js, React, Node.js, TypeScript, Shopify, and WordPress, the previous homepage framed the studio as an entry-level local brochure-site maker.
- **Unexploited Authority:** The domain hosts 160 production interactive web applications, demonstrating massive engineering depth, yet the homepage failed to leverage this proof against traditional agency competitors.

## 4. Preventing Keyword Cannibalization
- The homepage serves as the **Commercial Master Hub** targeting general and top-level vertical commercial terms.
- Specific deep sub-pages (`/services/takeaway-web-design-london`, `/tools/*`, `/blog/*`) target granular hyper-local and long-tail informational queries.
- The homepage links cleanly into service categories using descriptive semantic anchors, passing link equity and reinforcing topical hierarchy.

## 5. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) & AI Answer Strategy
To earn primary citations in **Google AI Overviews**, **ChatGPT Search**, and **Perplexity AI**:
1. **Direct-Answer Formulation:** Include direct, concise (40–60 word) definition blocks answering:
   - "What is custom web development?"
   - "How much does a commercial website cost in the UK?"
   - "Why choose Next.js over WordPress for commercial websites?"
2. **Tabular Comparison Data:** Machine-readable comparison tables highlighting speed, code ownership, ongoing retainers, and security vulnerability profiles.
3. **Multi-Entity JSON-LD Markup:** Combining `Organization`, `ProfessionalService`, `WebSite`, `WebPage`, `FAQPage`, and `Service` schemas with explicit `areaServed`, `founder`, `hasOfferCatalog`, and `sameAs` authority links.
