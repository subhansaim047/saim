    Consultant-Led Custom Suits

    A Digital Bespoke Suit Experience

    We bring you an old-world bespoke tailoring experience, guided by a personal consultant online. You’ll work one-on-one to choose your fabric, finalize your fit, and confirm every detail for your custom suit. Before production begins, we share clear 2D mockups for your approval so you know exactly what’s being made. Since day one, we’ve delivered a digital-first custom experience in 130+ countries, producing thousands of pieces with a 99% satisfaction rate for custom products. 

    Base Styles to Start Your Custom Suit

    Choose your foundation, then we tailor everything around it. From 2-piece and 3-piece suits to double-breasted styles, tuxedos, MAO suits, summer suits, and wedding suits, our offering covers every occasion and season. Each option is made to measure, customized to your preferences, and finished with the details that make it yours.

    OUR FABRIC LIBRARY

    Our fabric range covers every occasion and climate, sourced from leading mills including Vitale Barberis Canonico (VBC), Guabello, Ermenegildo Zegna, Loro Piana, Angelico, Liberty, Maurizo, Vito, and MB Sartorial. During your consultation, we recommend the right cloth for your needs, from Italian worsted wools (Super 110’s to 160’s) and superfine merino (120’s, 130’s, 150’s, plus ultra-fine 15 micron options) to natural-stretch performance wool, tropical and four-season weaves, Lycra stretch suiting, and breathable luxury linen, so your bespoke suit matches your season, occasion, and comfort preferences.

    Customization Options

    Every custom suit should look intentional. Choose the details that define the finish, from the buttons you touch every day to the lapel shape that frames your chest. We’ll guide you through each option during your consultation so your bespoke suit feels consistent from the outside in.

    Button Materials

    Buttons change the character of a tailored suit fast. Go classic, add shine, or keep it understated. We’ll help you match button tone to fabric color and formality.

    Popular Options:

    • Real Horn
    • Mother of Pearl
    • Wooden
    • Polyester

    Lapel Styles

    Lapels control the suit’s attitude. Notch stays versatile, peak adds presence, and shawl keeps it formal for eveningwear. We recommend based on your body type and the occasion.

    Popular Options:

    • Notch Lapel
    • Peak Lapel
    • Shawl Lapel

    Lining Materials

    The lining is where comfort meets personal style. Choose a smooth feel for easy wear, or a breathable blend for long days. It’s a key part of how made-to-measure suits feel on the body.

    Popular Options:

    • Satin
    • Cupro
    • Cotton Blend
    • Polyester Blend

    Monograms

    Keep it subtle and personal. A monogram adds identity without shouting. We place it where it looks clean and feels considered.

    Popular Options:

    • Inside Jackets
    • Underside Collar

    Our Construction Methods

    Choose how your tailored suit is built on the inside. Construction affects structure, drape, breathability, and how the suit ages over time. We use horsehair canvas in our canvas-built custom suits to create a clean chest shape, natural roll, and better breathability. It helps the jacket mold to you over time and keeps its structure without feeling stiff.

    Full Canvas Suits

     Canvas runs through the chest and shoulders for the most natural shape and long-term durability. Best for premium bespoke.

    Half Canvas Suits

    Canvas in the chest and shoulders with a lighter build elsewhere. A strong balance of structure, comfort, and value.

    Fused Suits

    Bonded interlining for a smooth front and faster build. Best for entry budgets and simpler use-cases.

    FAQ: Answered

    What’s the difference between bespoke and made to measure?

    Bespoke is built around your measurements and preferences with deeper control over details. Made to measure starts from a base pattern adjusted to your measurements.

    How do I get measured if I’m not visiting a store?

    You can use our detailed at-home measurement guide. A consultant reviews your inputs before we proceed.


    How many fittings or revisions are involved?

    Most orders are finalized through measurement review and mockup approval. If needed, we guide minor adjustments and free alterations decided by your design consultant.

    Do you guarantee the fit?

    Yes. We offer a perfect fit and size guarantee, with clear support to get it right

    What are your payment terms?

    We require advance payment, with the option for installments. Full payment is due before shipping.

    Can I customize every detail of the suit?

    Yes. You can customize fabric, fit, lapels, lining, buttons, pockets, and finishing details.

    What construction methods do you offer?

    We offer full canvas and half canvas options using horsehair canvas, plus fused construction for entry builds.

    What fabrics do you offer?

    We carry a curated range across wool, merino, linen, stretch suiting, and travel-friendly options sourced from renowned mills

    What’s the production and delivery timeline?

    Timelines vary by fabric and construction. Share your date and we’ll recommend the most realistic route. Average turnaround is 4 weeks.

    What if I’m not sure what style to choose?

    Tell us your occasion, climate, and preference for structure vs comfort. We’ll recommend the best base style, fit, and fabric direction.

    Can I order just a jacket or trousers, not a full suit?

    Yes. You can order a separate jacket or trousers, tailored to the same measurement profile.

    Do you offer fabric swatches before I decide?

    Yes. For select fabrics, we can arrange swatches so you can check color and texture first.

    Will the suit match the mockup exactly?

    The mockup confirms design details and placements. Fabric tone can vary slightly by screen, but we guide you to the closest match.

    What if I’m between sizes or have an athletic build?

    That’s exactly where made-to-measure works best. We tailor shoulders, chest, waist, and sleeve balance to your proportions.


    Can you match a suit I already own?

    Yes. Share photos and fit notes, and we’ll build a tailored suit with the same silhouette or improve it where needed.
