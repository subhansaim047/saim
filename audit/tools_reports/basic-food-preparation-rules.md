## 3. Gap Analysis & Differentiators

- **Station-Adaptive Rules:** None of the competitors adapt rules to specific stations. Our tool allows staff to toggle between Master Kitchen, Raw Meat & Poultry, Veg & Salad Sanitising, Cookline & Hot-Holding, and Dedicated Allergen-Safe Zone.
- **Interactive Pre-Service Audit:** Live 8-point checklist allowing head chefs to audit kitchen prep readiness with an instant compliance status.
- **UK vs Scotland Temperature Standards:** One-click toggle between UK Standard (75°C core) and Food Hygiene (Scotland) Regulations 2006 (82°C core).
- **100% Free & Printable:** Generates an A4 print layout with premises name, bench ID, and supervisor sign-off without forcing account creation.
