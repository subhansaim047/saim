## 2. Data We Collect & How It Is Obtained

We process personal data only when strictly necessary:

- **Project Enquiries:** Name, business name, email address, website URL, budget range, and project specifications submitted via our contact forms or brief submission.
- **Direct Communications:** Message contents and contact details provided when you communicate directly via WhatsApp (+92 319 6526604) or Instagram (@saim\_agency).
- **Interactive Tools:** Data entered into our free kitchen tools (such as the Allergen Matrix, Food Cost Calculator, and Recipe Calculator) is stored entirely in your local browser storage (` localStorage `) and is **never transmitted to our servers**.
- **Technical Access Logs:** IP address, device type, and timestamp metadata automatically recorded by our cloud hosting infrastructure (Vercel) to protect service availability and prevent security attacks.

## 3. Cookies & Local Browser Storage

We do not use third-party tracking or advertising cookies. Our free client-side kitchen tools and compliance notice use local browser storage strictly to preserve your work and preferences locally on your device:

- saim\_cookie\_consent: Remembers your cookie notice preference so the notice is not repeatedly shown.
- saim\_allergen\_matrix\_v1: Saves dish and allergen data in your browser for the Allergen Matrix Generator.
- saim\_food\_cost\_v1: Saves recipe pricing and margin calculations locally on your device.
- saim\_recipe\_calc\_v1: Saves scaled commercial batch catering measurements locally.

For our complete technical storage inventory and details on managing browser storage, view our  [Cookie Policy](https://www.saimdev.site/cookie-policy).
