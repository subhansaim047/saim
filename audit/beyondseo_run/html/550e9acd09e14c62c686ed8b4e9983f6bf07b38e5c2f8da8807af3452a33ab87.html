Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up a direct takeaway ordering system.

Q1.How does the direct ordering system work without aggregator commissions?
When a customer visits your custom website, they browse your digital menu with live allergen filters, add items to their basket, and check out directly via WhatsApp or direct SMS. The complete order—including customer name, delivery address, phone number, order items, and allergen notes—lands instantly on your kitchen phone or tablet. You keep 100% of the sale with zero commission cuts.
Q2.Why is an online allergen filter essential for UK takeaways?
Under the Food Information Regulations 2014 and Natasha's Law, food businesses must provide clear, verified allergen information for all 14 statutory allergens. Our menus feature an interactive filter allowing customers with allergies (such as gluten, nuts, dairy, or shellfish) to filter dishes safely before ordering, drastically reducing front-of-house telephone queries and cross-contamination liability.
Q3.Are there any hidden monthly fees or subscription charges?
No. You pay a single fixed fee of £250 for the complete website setup and deployment. You own the code and domain. Hosting on high-speed global infrastructure is included free under standard usage tiers, meaning zero recurring monthly cuts from your hard-earned profits.
Q4.How quickly can my takeaway website launch?
Standard takeaway websites are completed and launched within 3 to 5 working days once you provide your menu, pricing, logo, and food photos. We handle domain setup, digital menu transcription, allergen tagging, and Google Maps local SEO integration.
Q5.Can customers still pay online via card or Apple Pay / Google Pay?
Yes. If you wish to accept digital payments before dispatch, we can integrate Stripe or PayPal directly into your checkout workflow, allowing instant card payments directly into your UK bank account at standard card processing rates (around 1.4% + 20p) instead of aggregator 30%+ cuts.
