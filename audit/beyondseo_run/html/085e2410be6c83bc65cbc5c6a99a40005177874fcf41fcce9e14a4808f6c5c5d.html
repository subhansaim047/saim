2. Data We Collect & How It Is Obtained

We process personal data only when strictly necessary:

  • Project Enquiries: Name, business name, email address, website URL, budget range, and project specifications submitted via our contact forms or brief submission.
  • Direct Communications: Message contents and contact details provided when you communicate directly via WhatsApp (+92 319 6526604) or Instagram (@saim_agency).

  • Technical Access Logs: IP address, device type, and timestamp metadata automatically recorded by our cloud hosting infrastructure (Vercel) to protect service availability and prevent security attacks.

3. Cookies & Local Browser Storage

We do not use third-party tracking or advertising cookies. Our free client-side kitchen tools and compliance notice use local browser storage strictly to preserve your work and preferences locally on your device:

  • saim_cookie_consent: Remembers your cookie notice preference so the notice is not repeatedly shown.
  • saim_allergen_matrix_v1: Saves dish and allergen data in your browser for the Allergen Matrix Generator.
  • saim_food_cost_v1: Saves recipe pricing and margin calculations locally on your device.
  • saim_recipe_calc_v1: Saves scaled commercial batch catering measurements locally.

For our complete technical storage inventory and details on managing browser storage, view our Cookie Policy.
